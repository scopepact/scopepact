ScopePact uses established licenses for two distinct public layers.

- `crates/core/` and `crates/guard/`;
- the root Cargo workspace manifest and lockfile.

The complete terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and
[`LICENSES/MPL-2.0.txt`](LICENSES/MPL-2.0.txt). MPL-2.0 keeps distributed
modifications to covered files available while permitting ScopePact to be
combined with separate open or proprietary modules.

## Apache-2.0 Open Protocol

Unless a file carries a more specific notice, the following are licensed under
the Apache License 2.0:

- `crates/protocol/`, `docs/`, `conformance/`, `sdks/`, `scripts/`, and `examples/`;
- `.github/`;

The complete terms are in [`LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`](LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt).
