# ScopePact Community Charter

ScopePact is a community-first open protocol project. It exists to make agent
authority safer, portable, and independently verifiable, not to collect rent
from a shared trust layer.

## What Winning Means

The project succeeds when:

- independent teams can implement the protocol from public materials;
- more runtimes and protocols can exchange bounded authority safely;
- conformance and adversarial evidence become broader and more credible;
- implementers from different backgrounds can understand and challenge the
  security model;
- contributors earn responsibility through public work and sound judgment.

Repository revenue, token value, or control of downstream users are not project
success metrics.

## Community-First Commitments

The official ScopePact project does not sell:

- permission to implement or use the protocol;
- access to normative specifications, schemas, or conformance vectors;
- governance votes, maintainer roles, or roadmap priority;
- favorable conformance outcomes, compatibility badges, or security claims;
- private early access to normative protocol decisions.

Commercial use is welcome under the repository licenses. Companies may offer
hosting, integration, support, audits, or other products around ScopePact, but
payment does not create authority over the public protocol.

The project does not currently solicit donations or sponsorships. If community
funding is considered later, its terms, conflicts, and use must be public. It
must fund openly available work and cannot purchase exclusive protocol
features or decision-making power.

## How To Participate

You do not need to be a cryptographer or protocol expert.

- **Use it:** run the Guard or conformance workbench and report where the
  experience is unclear.
- **Implement it:** add a runtime, adapter, vector, verifier, policy example, or
  integration profile.
- **Challenge it:** review transcripts, identify ambiguous behavior, build
  rejection cases, and test threat assumptions.
- **Explain it:** improve examples, diagrams, terminology, accessibility, and
  translations without weakening the security claims.
- **Steward it:** review contributions, facilitate decisions, and help new
  contributors become effective.

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), ask open-ended questions in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/scopepact/scopepact/discussions), and
use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/scopepact/scopepact/issues) for concrete
bugs and scoped work. Suspected vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
not a public issue.

## Community Standards

Protocol decisions are argued from threat models, interoperability evidence,
and executable tests. Status, employment, sponsorship, and commercial interest
do not make a technical claim stronger. Contributors disclose material
conflicts, preserve reasoned dissent in the public record, and follow the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

The maintainer path and decision process are documented in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) and [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
