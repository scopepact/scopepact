# ScopePact Trademark Policy

The ScopePact name and project logos identify the official project and are not
licensed by the repository's copyright licenses. This initial policy is meant
to prevent confusion while allowing accurate community discussion.

## Uses That Do Not Require Permission

- referring to the official ScopePact project or linking to it;
- stating accurately that software interoperates with or is derived from
  ScopePact;
- redistributing an unmodified official release with its original notices;
- using the name in news, education, research, reviews, and community events
  without suggesting endorsement.

## Uses That Require Permission

- using ScopePact or a confusingly similar term in the name of a modified
  distribution, product, company, domain, or hosted service;
- using the official logo for a fork or third-party product;
- describing a product as official, approved, or certified without written
  authorization;
- using an official compatibility badge without satisfying its published
  conformance requirements.

Modified distributions must use a clearly distinct primary name. They may say
"based on ScopePact" or "compatible with ScopePact" when that statement is
accurate. Names such as "ScopePact2" or "ScopePact Pro" are not permitted for
unapproved forks because they imply continuity with the official project.

Trademark rights and registration status vary by jurisdiction. This policy
does not waive rights provided by applicable law and should receive
professional review before a stable public launch.
