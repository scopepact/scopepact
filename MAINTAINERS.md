# Maintainers

ScopePact is currently in bootstrap governance.

- [@wilson0506](https://github.com/wilson0506) - bootstrap maintainer and
  repository steward

The bootstrap maintainer has no special claim over the protocol standard. The
role exists to keep reviews, releases, security response, and community
operations moving until responsibility is shared by a broader maintainer group.

## Becoming A Maintainer

Maintainers are selected from contributors who have demonstrated, over time:

- sustained public contributions or reviews;
- careful reasoning about security boundaries and compatibility;
- constructive work across disagreements;
- support for contributors with different experience levels;
- disclosure and responsible handling of conflicts of interest.

An existing maintainer or active contributor may nominate a candidate in a
public issue. The nomination should link to relevant work and remain open for
community comment for at least 14 days. Active maintainers decide by consensus
and record the outcome and material objections publicly.

Maintainers may step down at any time. A maintainer may be removed for extended
inactivity, repeated failure to disclose conflicts, abuse of project access, or
serious Code of Conduct violations. Except when immediate access removal is
needed for safety, removal follows the same public notice and review process.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

- protect protocol compatibility and the documented security boundary;
- review contributions on evidence rather than affiliation or funding;
- keep normative decisions, release criteria, and conformance results public;
- recuse from decisions involving a material personal or commercial conflict;
- make space for additional qualified maintainers and independent implementers.
